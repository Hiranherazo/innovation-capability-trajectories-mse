# Innovation Capability Trajectories in Micro and Small Enterprises

This repository contains the computational materials associated with the study:

** Innovation Capability Trajectories in Micro and Small Enterprises: A Microfoundational Explanation Based on Individual Innovation Capability Development **

## Project Overview

The project investigates how organizational innovation capability develops within Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) through the interaction of:

•	Organizational Support
•	Governance
•	Innovation Culture Contagion
•	Potential Well-Being (PWB)
•	Innovation Profile (IP)
•	Time Allocation Decisions
•	Individual Innovation Capability (Ladvantage)

The study employs Agent-Based Simulation (ABS) implemented in NetLogo 6.4.0 and statistical analyses conducted in R.

The simulation identifies three recurring innovation capability trajectories:

•	Accumulation
•	Sustaining
•	Erosion

The simulation examines the mechanisms through which organizational innovation capability emerges from the accumulation of individual innovation capabilities.

## Simulation Logic

The model follows the behavioral sequence:

Organizational Support + Governance + Innovation Culture Contagion
↓
Potential Well-Being (PWB)
↓
Innovation Profile (IP)
↓
Time Allocation Decisions
↓
Ladvantage (Individual Innovation Capability)
↓
Organizational Innovation Capability
