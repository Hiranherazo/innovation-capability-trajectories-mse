# Figures
 
This folder contains the figures associated with the study:
 
"Innovation Capability Trajectories in Micro and Small Enterprises: A Microfoundational Explanation Based on Individual Innovation Capability Development"
 
## Contents
 
### Figure 1
 
Conceptual Framework of Innovation Capability Development in Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs).
 
The figure illustrates the relationships among:
 
- Organizational Support
- Governance
- Innovation Culture Contagion
- Potential Well-Being (PWB)
- Innovation Profile (IP)
- Time Allocation Decisions
- Individual Innovation Capability (Ladvantage)
- Organizational Innovation Capability
 
### Figure 2
 
Innovation Capability Development Trajectories.
 
The figure presents the accumulation, sustaining, and erosion trajectories identified through the analysis of simulation outputs.
 
## Purpose
 
The figures support the interpretation of the model structure and simulation results reported in the associated manuscript.
 
## Reproducibility
 
The figures were generated from the conceptual framework and the statistical analyses conducted using NetLogo simulation outputs and R-based analytical procedures.
